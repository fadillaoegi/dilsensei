# Naskah video demo — 2 menit

Aturan lomba: maksimal 2 menit, publik di YouTube atau Vimeo, harus menampilkan
app berjalan di perangkat yang dituju, dan **tanpa musik berhak cipta**.
Juri tidak wajib menonton lebih dari dua menit, jadi hal terpenting harus muncul
di **15 detik pertama**.

Rekam dalam Bahasa Inggris. Setel bahasa app ke English sebelum merekam.

---

## Aturan produksi

- Rekam layar HP langsung (Android screen recorder), potret, 1080×2400 atau lebih.
- Matikan notifikasi lain, aktifkan mode jangan ganggu.
- Isi progres dulu: selesaikan dua sesi dan **sengaja salah pada beberapa butir
  partikel**, supaya Peta Kelemahan punya isi saat direkam.
- Nyalakan dev toggle Pro sebelum merekam bagian premium, lalu **matikan** saat
  merekam bagian paywall.
- Musik: tanpa musik, atau musik bebas lisensi. Narasi suaramu sendiri lebih baik
  daripada musik.

## Naskah penuh untuk dibaca

> Knowing a Japanese grammar rule is not the same as saying it in three seconds.
> DilSensei trains that gap.
>
> Before drilling, the full kana charts are free — no session, no subscription.
>
> A session is short. Every answer is timed, because hesitation is the thing being
> trained. Get it wrong and the item does not disappear; it comes back later in the
> same session, so you never leave having only failed.
>
> Mistakes are not recorded as wrong. They are recorded as patterns — here, the
> place particle.
>
> The summary is a diagnosis, not a score screen. The reflex score combines
> accuracy with how long you hesitated.
>
> On the weak-spot map, recent mistakes weigh more than old ones. And answers that
> are correct but slow still count, because reflex means fast. Two different
> problems are shown differently.
>
> One tap builds a session containing only the patterns I am still weak at. That
> loop is the product.
>
> The free tier gives one session a day and a preview of the map. Pro unlocks the
> full diagnosis, unlimited sessions, and history.
>
> DilSensei. Grammar you don't have to think about.

Durasi bacaan santai naskah ini sekitar 1 menit 50 detik, jadi masih ada ruang.

## Kalau notifikasi ikut ditampilkan

Sisipkan 8 detik antara 1:35 dan 1:45: nyalakan pengingat di Pengaturan, setel
jamnya satu menit ke depan, lalu tunggu notifikasinya muncul.

> "The daily reminder names the pattern you are weakest at, not a generic nudge —
> and it skips itself entirely if you already practised today."

Ini berguna kalau kamu mengejar kategori OneSignal, tapi jangan sampai video
melewati dua menit demi shot ini.
