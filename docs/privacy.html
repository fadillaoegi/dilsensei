Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

The short version. DilSensei has no accounts and no sign-in. Your learning progress stays on your device. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. We do use Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous usage events, with advertising-ID collection switched off. Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled by your own device and involve no server.

1. Who we are

DilSensei ("the app") is a Japanese grammar drill application published by FL Dev ("we", "us"). You can reach us at fadilfdl2710@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally in the app's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • The nickname you type during onboarding.
  • Your learning goal and daily practice target.
  • Your practice progress: streak, number of completed sessions, best reflex score, and how often each grammar pattern was missed.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never receive it.

3. Information processed when you subscribe

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play together with RevenueCat, Inc., which manages purchase validation on our behalf. When you start a trial or subscribe, RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous app user identifier generated on your device. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Purchase and subscription details, such as the product purchased, purchase date, renewal date, and trial status.
  • Basic technical data about your device and app version, and your country as reported by the store.

We use this only to unlock premium content and to understand overall subscription performance. We never receive your payment card details; payment is processed entirely by Google Play. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

4. Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used in aggregate. It records events such as completing onboarding, finishing a practice session, opening the paywall, and the outcome of a purchase attempt, together with a Firebase-generated installation identifier and basic device and country information.

Two limits we set deliberately: advertising-ID collection is disabled in the app manifest, and we never attach your nickname, your lesson answers, or any other content you type to an analytics event.

Analytics collection is enabled only in released builds. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

5. Practice reminders

You can switch on a daily practice reminder in Settings. The reminder is scheduled and shown entirely by your own device using Android's notification system. It does not involve any server, and we do not receive a push token or any delivery information.

The reminder text may name the grammar pattern you are currently weakest at, for example "Place particle is still slipping". That text is composed on your device from data that never leaves it. Android may display it on your lock screen, so turn the reminder off if you would rather it not appear there.

The reminder is off by default and asks for notification permission only when you turn it on. Denying the permission leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No crash reporting SDK.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.
  • No account, so no password to be leaked.

7. Internet access

Lesson content and fonts are bundled inside the app, so practice works offline. The app connects to the internet to verify your subscription status, to load subscription prices on the paywall, and to send the anonymous usage events described in section 4.

8. Children

DilSensei is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can erase everything we store by deleting the app or clearing its data in Android settings.
  • You can request deletion of purchase records associated with your anonymous identifier by emailing us. Please include the order ID from your Google Play receipt so we can locate the record.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Data retention

Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat and Google Play for as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

11. Changes to this policy

If we add features that process new data, such as server-sent push notifications or crash reporting, we will update this page and change the date above before the feature is released.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: fadilfdl2710@gmail.com.
