Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

The short version. DilSensei has no accounts and no sign-in. Your learning progress stays on your device. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not run any analytics or tracking SDK.

1. Who we are

DilSensei ("the app") is a Japanese grammar drill application published by FL Dev ("we", "us"). You can reach us at REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally in the app's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • The nickname you type during onboarding.
  • Your learning goal and daily practice target.
  • Your practice progress: streak, number of completed sessions, best reflex score, and how often each grammar pattern was missed.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never receive it.

3. Information processed when you subscribe

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play together with RevenueCat, Inc., which manages purchase validation on our behalf. When you start a trial or subscribe, RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous app user identifier generated on your device. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Purchase and subscription details, such as the product purchased, purchase date, renewal date, and trial status.
  • Basic technical data about your device and app version, and your country as reported by the store.

We use this only to unlock premium content and to understand overall subscription performance. We never receive your payment card details; payment is processed entirely by Google Play. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, attribution, or crash reporting SDK.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.
  • No account, so no password to be leaked.

5. Internet access

Lesson content and fonts are bundled inside the app, so practice works offline. The app connects to the internet only to verify your subscription status and to load subscription prices on the paywall.

6. Children

DilSensei is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can erase everything we store by deleting the app or clearing its data in Android settings.
  • You can request deletion of purchase records associated with your anonymous identifier by emailing us. Please include the order ID from your Google Play receipt so we can locate the record.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Data retention

Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat and Google Play for as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

9. Changes to this policy

If we add features that process new data, such as push notifications, we will update this page and change the date above before the feature is released.

10. Contact
